# START HERE — TitleChain Foundation + ICSN

Welcome to the public community for **TitleChain Foundation** and **ICSN — Internet Cooperative for Sovereign Networks**.

You do not have to be a developer to contribute. Standards need people who understand technology, economics, statistics, privacy, law, accounting, design, accessibility, education, security, governance, business, and human behavior.

## What is what?

### TitleChain Foundation

TitleChain Foundation provides long-term stewardship for public-interest work. Its commitments include human authority, meaningful consent, portability, recovery, the right to exit, open review, and protection against sponsor or vendor capture.

### ICSN

ICSN develops open standards for human authority, accountable agents, identity, credentials, provenance, privacy, interoperability, and sovereign digital systems. You may read, review, fork, or independently implement the public work without enrolling in M5.

### M5

M5 is one implementation and activation environment used to test and demonstrate parts of the architecture. **M5 does not own the public ICSN standards.**

## How public work moves

**Discussion → Issue → contribution → independent review → evidence → release**

Discussions are where people arrive, ask, explore, and help define a need. Issues turn sufficiently clear needs into bounded work. A contribution may be code, documentation, research, testing, design, governance review, curriculum, security analysis, or another form of useful evidence.

## Public safety

Do not post credentials, identity documents, private participant data, wallet or financial information, confidential business information, production secrets, private repository material, or unreported security vulnerabilities.

Public participation does not create employment, compensation, funding, organization membership, an M5 account, a credential, governance authority, or standards approval.

**Human authority first. Fund the commons. Never own the commons.**
