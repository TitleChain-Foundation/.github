# ANNOUNCEMENT — Power of Her Purse + 100K Women

**September 3, 2026**

TitleChain Foundation is opening the next phase of its public work. Our first scaled activation goal is to help support **up to 100,000 women-led ventures** as AI changes how people learn, work, build businesses, and participate in the digital economy.

We call the initiative **Power of Her Purse**.

> **Her life. Her AI. Her knowledge. Her work. Her money. Her future.**

This is not another AI class. We begin with the human being before her employer, résumé, job title, or AI vendor.

The aim is to give each participant a human-controlled starting environment for identity, knowledge, skills, personal AI, and economic participation, then allow her to decide what she wants to learn, connect, build, teach, or earn.

**I AM → I KNOW → I CAN → I DID → I CAN TEACH → I CAN EARN**

## Why women first?

Women own approximately **14.2 million U.S. businesses generating approximately $2.8 trillion in receipts**. At the same time, AI is rapidly changing employment, entrepreneurship, consulting, professional services, and business operations.

The goal is for women to enter that transition with greater agency over their identity, knowledge, AI, and economic choices, rather than simply becoming another account inside someone else's platform.

The 100,000 figure is a goal to support **up to 100,000 women-led ventures**. It is not a claim of completed enrollment, guaranteed funding, or current activation at that scale.

## Fund the activation. Never own the member.

Sponsors may support activation and receive appropriate recognition and aggregate program outcomes. They do not purchase a participant's private data, private M5Brain content, personal-agent conversations, private credentials, financial information, confidential business information, standards authority, or control over a participant's technology choices.

The corresponding public-standards principle is: **Fund the commons. Never own the commons.**

## Public standards remain public

TitleChain Foundation stewards ICSN. M5 is one implementation and activation environment; it does not own the public standard. You do not have to enroll in M5 to read, review, fork, or independently implement public ICSN work.
