# PARTICIPATE — Contribute, Sponsor, Sponsor an Engineer

There is more than one way to help build public infrastructure.

You do not need an M5 account, a funded role, GitHub organization membership, or a software-engineering background to participate in public discussion or standards work.

## Contribute expertise

We welcome developers, engineers, cybersecurity and privacy specialists, identity and distributed-systems specialists, economists, statisticians, designers, accessibility reviewers, educators, trainers, legal and governance reviewers, accountants, business operators, researchers, professional associations, and subject-matter experts.

Start with one bounded action:

1. [Choose a contribution pathway](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/CONTRIBUTOR-PATHWAYS.md).
2. [Browse open Issues](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/issues).
3. Comment on an Issue before beginning substantial work.
4. Contribute one focused review, test, document, recommendation, schema, or pull request.
5. A responsible reviewer evaluates the evidence before acceptance.

Have an early idea or do not know where you fit? Ask in [Contributor Q&A](https://github.com/orgs/TitleChain-Foundation/discussions/categories/questions-help).

## Sponsor public work

Individuals can contribute at small-dollar levels through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/TitleChain-Foundation). Organizations may support open specifications, tooling, documentation, security, privacy, contributor coordination, reference implementations, or activation programs.

Funding does not purchase standards authority, governance control, preferential technical treatment, conformance approval, private participant data, or exclusive access to people receiving support.

## Sponsor an engineer

Companies can contribute sustained technical capacity through two public-interest pathways:

### Corporate Engineering Secondment

A company continues employing an engineer while assigning approved time to a defined TitleChain Foundation or ICSN public workstream.

### Sponsored Maintainer Fellowship

A company underwrites an engineer or maintainer contributing sustained work to approved public infrastructure.

In-kind support should be documented by people, hours, duration, scope, and deliverables. It is reported separately from cash support. Funding never purchases control of the standards.
