## What useful contribution looks like

A contribution can be:

- a threat model, privacy review, or accessibility test;
- a reproducible implementation or conformance finding;
- standards, legal, economic, accounting, or governance analysis;
- a schema, test, documentation improvement, or pull request;
- curriculum, trainer guidance, user research, or design feedback; or
- public evidence that confirms, challenges, or improves a requirement.

Use the [Contributor Pathways](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/CONTRIBUTOR-PATHWAYS.md) to match your skills to work. If no current Issue fits, use [Contributor Q&A](https://github.com/orgs/TitleChain-Foundation/discussions/categories/questions-help) to describe what you know and what you want to help with.

## Standards and implementation

ICSN contains public specifications, governance, schemas, and conformance work. M5 is one implementation and activation environment used to test parts of the architecture. An M5 implementation decision does not automatically become an ICSN standard; public standards move through review and evidence.

Independent implementers are welcome. No M5 enrollment is required to read, review, fork, test, or implement ICSN work.

## First activation and the path toward 100K

The first supported cohort prioritizes **75 women**. The broader project activation group brings the first activation to **100 total participants**. This phase is intended to test the human, technical, privacy, curriculum, contributor, and economic pathways needed before responsible scale. It is a learning and activation pathway, not a promise of selection, funding, employment, certification, or a completed cohort.

The broader Power of Her Purse goal is to support **up to 100,000 women-led ventures**. Progress must be demonstrated through real participation, accepted work, evidence, trainer capacity, funding, and accountable outcomes rather than audience claims.
