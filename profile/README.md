# TitleChain Foundation

**Open standards and public reference infrastructure for portable, interoperable, and owner-controlled identity, credentials, data, agents, records, and authorized digital assets.**

TitleChain Foundation stewards public specifications and review processes designed to help people and legal entities retain authority over what belongs to them, how it may be used, and which humans, agents, tools, or services may act on their behalf.

Our public standards program is **ICSN — Internet Cooperative for Sovereign Networks**. ICSN develops implementation-neutral specifications for human-rooted identity, portable credentials, meaningful consent, recovery, provenance, accountable delegation to software and AI agents, and conformance across independent implementations.

## The public core

### Sovereign identity at no charge to the individual

The Foundation's first public-infrastructure commitment is a human-controlled IAM identity origin offered at no charge to the individual and designed to preserve foundational access over time.

The architecture supports progressive and plural evidence pathways rather than assuming every person begins with the same institutional documents. Self-asserted origins, member-approved attestations, portable learning and work evidence, and optional institutional or jurisdictional credentials can participate according to their stated assurance and legal context.

An IAM identity origin does not by itself establish citizenship, immigration status, regulated identity verification, eligibility for a financial service, or authority to represent another person or legal entity.

- **[Access the no-charge IAM starting point](https://m5bank.app/)**
- **[Read the Public Infrastructure Commitment](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/PUBLIC-INFRASTRUCTURE-COMMITMENT.md)**

GitHub participation never requires IAM, M5 membership, or program enrollment.

### A free, standalone capability profile

The blank M5-CV / M5isYOU capability template is a public design for a standalone capability record. The Foundation's commitment is to release an expressly open-license version that anyone may use, copy, adapt, or improve without requiring an M5 connection. It helps a person describe what she knows, what she can do, what she has done, what she can teach, and what evidence she chooses to present.

Until that open-license designation is published in the standards repository, the current asset-level license matrix controls reuse of the public-review template.

Connecting a capability profile to IAM, credentials, M5, or another compatible implementation is optional and requires a separate affirmative choice. Connected services must respect the applicable consent, privacy, portability, correction, export, withdrawal, and revocation boundaries.

### M5Core and the Freedom Office reference environment

M5 is one optional implementation environment used to test how the public principles work in practice. Its developing core connects:

```text
no-charge IAM identity origin
        ↓
portable capability profile and evidence
        ↓
member-controlled data, knowledge, and context
        ↓
Freedom Office / M5Shell
        ↓
human-authorized M5Agents
        ↓
documented MCP and OpenAPI integrations
        ↓
learning, business, and operating workflows
        ↓
member-controlled provenance and outcomes
```

The goal is a portable working environment in which models, applications, integrations, and service providers can change while the person or legal entity retains control of identity, data, credentials, permissions, records, and authorized assets.

Finance, accounting, ledgers, payments, social systems, registries, education, communications, and other services may connect through documented interfaces. They are optional integration layers, not mandatory dependencies of the public standards or standalone capability profile.

M5 does not own ICSN, and an M5 implementation does not become a standard merely because it is deployed. Reusable lessons must enter the ordinary public RFC, review, security, privacy, and conformance process.

## From the first 100 to 100,000 women-led ventures

The first scaled activation goal is to support **up to 100,000 women-led ventures** with a sovereign digital starting point and a pathway to learn, build, prove, operate, teach, and earn without surrendering ownership of identity, credentials, business memory, or standards governance.

The first 100 members are the reference learning and activation environment:

```text
I AM → I KNOW → I CAN → I DID → I CAN TEACH → I CAN EARN
```

Members may activate their own Freedom Office, learn to operate authorized agents, apply capabilities to real work, create or strengthen a business, and—if qualified—help train and activate additional members and businesses. The goal is a distributed network of operators, professionals, developers, educators, trainers, and entrepreneurs rather than permanent dependence on one centralized platform.

- **[Read the 100K Women-Led Venture Activation plan](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/initiatives/100K-WOMEN-LED-VENTURES.md)**

## Constitutional stewardship

**Built for stewardship, not acquisition.**

The Foundation is structured to preserve long-term, human-first public stewardship while separating constitutional governance from commercial implementation. Its purpose-trust model is designed for continuity, independence, and resistance to capture.

The public commitments are simple:

- human authority remains the root;
- privacy and meaningful consent are structural requirements;
- people and legal entities retain portability, recovery, and the right to exit;
- funding, adoption, vendor status, or implementation scale do not create unilateral control;
- coercive surveillance and forced vendor lock-in are inconsistent with the mission; and
- M5 is an implementation environment, not the owner of the standard.

**[Read the Trust & Non-Capture summary](https://github.com/TitleChain-Foundation/icsn-standards/blob/main/TRUST-AND-NON-CAPTURE.md).** The public summary does not replace the Trust instrument, Foundation governing documents, or applicable law.
